Cannabis Product Schema

A jurisdiction-neutral interchange format for producers, marketplaces, and retailers. Core is representable-everything. Profiles narrow it.

What this is not

  • Not "the standard." Cannabis Product Schema is one schema, maintained by one company. It doesn't speak for the industry and makes no claim to.
  • Not an alliance, consortium, or working group. See GOVERNANCE.md — there is no shared governance body behind this repository.
  • Not legal advice. Jurisdiction profiles encode our best reading of cited primary rule text at a point in time. Verify against current law and your own counsel before relying on them for compliance.

Core v0.1

  • product.schema.json — Product is what it is
  • package.schema.json — Package is the unit
  • lab-result.schema.json — LabResult wins COA/potency
  • label.schema.json — Label is display copy
  • party.schema.json — Party is brand, producer, laboratory, processor

Profiles

  • us-ga-hemp — profile.json, product.profile.schema.json, lab-result.profile.schema.json

Georgia consumable-hemp retail; core has no jurisdiction constants.
